# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

## Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

### Approach

Track the minimum price seen so far and compute the best profit for each later day.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

This is the standard single-pass solution that keeps the lowest purchase price and updates the maximum profit.
